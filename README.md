# 📱 Phone & PC for SillyTavern

An immersion extension that gives `{{user}}` an in-story **phone** (or **PC**). It's full of apps: texting, calls, social media, money, shopping, food delivery, dating and more. You can also peek at what characters get up to online.

- **Everything you do waits for your reply.** Texts, payments, orders, calls and posts you make on the device are **queued** and only delivered when you send your next chat message. Then they're injected into the prompt, so you can write the text and the rest of your reply together.
- **Characters use their phones too.** They text you, call, send money, order you gifts and post online through small tags in their replies. The tags are removed from the chat and show up on the phone with notifications.
- **Or roleplay entirely through the phone.** Switch a chat to **phone-only roleplay** and the whole story happens by text: your messages send instantly and characters answer by text (see [Phone-only roleplay](#phone-only-roleplay)).
- **Peek at their phones.** Each app fills up with a whole feed: a character's X, Instagram or Reddit profile (10+ posts), their search and browsing history, and their music, plus payments, orders, location and dating profile. **Load more** keeps scrolling back in time. Everything is written in character and based on the story so far.
- **Your own feeds.** X, Instagram and Reddit each have a **Feed** tab: your home timeline, with posts from story characters mixed in with friends, strangers, celebrities and news.
- **Real photos.** Selfies, Instagram posts, products, food and dating profiles can be generated by whatever image generator you use (see [Photos](#photos)).

## Apps

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 💬 **Messages** | Text anyone. Send photos. Read receipts. ↩ quotes a text into your chat reply. |
| 📞 **Phone** | Recent calls, missed calls, voicemail transcripts, contacts. Calling someone connects with your next reply. |
| 𝕏 **X**, 📸 **Instagram**, 👽 **Reddit** | **Feed**: your home timeline (story characters mixed with everyone else), with refresh and load more. **Profiles**: peek at anyone's whole profile feed, then load older posts. Tap a name to open their profile. Post as yourself. |
| 🧭 **Browser** | A character's private search and browsing history. |
| 🎵 **Music** | What they're listening to. |
| 💸 **Pocket** | A Venmo/Cash App-style wallet: balance, pay, request, pay or decline requests, a friends feed of other people's payments, add cash. |
| 🛒 **Cartly** | Search an online store (results fit your story's world), buy for yourself or send a gift with a note, and track the delivery. |
| 🍔 **Munch** | Food delivery: restaurants, menus, cart, tip, deliver to yourself or someone else, live tracking. |
| 🔥 **Spark** | Dating app: swipe on generated locals or on story characters who are on the app, get matches, and chat. |
| 📍 **Locate** | Find-my-friends map showing where people are and what they're doing. |
| 🖼️ **Photos** | Every photo you've sent and received. |
| 📝 **Notes** | Private notes. Characters never see them. |
| ⭐ **Rated** *(18+, off by default)* | An anonymous app where adults post photos of themselves to be rated 1–10. Rate posts, post yourself, read comments, and maybe spot a character who's secretly posting. |
| ⚙️ **Settings** | Phone or PC, dark/light/auto theme, wallpapers (including a custom image), starting balance. |

Deliveries move forward as the story does (food arrives within a few messages, packages take longer), and characters are told when something arrives at their door.

## Install

1. In SillyTavern, open **Extensions** (the stacked-blocks icon) → **Install extension**.
2. Paste `https://github.com/howardsierra/SillyTavernPhone-And-PC` and install.
3. A phone button appears on the right side of the screen. You can drag it anywhere. The phone is also in the **wand menu** (✨), and you can use `/phone` (`/phone pay`, `/phone pc`, `/phone close`…).

## How characters use the phone

The model is taught these tags. You can edit the instructions under **Extensions → Phone & PC → Prompts**.

```html
<sms from="Lena">hey, you up?
can't sleep</sms>                                         <!-- each line = one bubble -->
<sms from="Lena" image="mirror selfie in a hoodie">thoughts?</sms>
<call from="Lena" status="missed">hey it's me, call me back</call>
<post app="x|instagram|reddit" from="Lena" image="..." title="..." sub="r/...">text</post>
<search from="Lena">how to tell someone you like them</search>
<pay from="Lena" to="{{user}}" amount="20" note="🍕 pizza"/>
<request from="Lena" to="{{user}}" amount="12.50" note="🧺 picnic"/>
<order app="food|shop" from="Lena" for="{{user}}" item="Honey cakes" price="18" store="Bramble Bakery">eat something!</order>
<location from="Lena" place="Moonlit Lake">skipping stones</location>
<sms app="spark" from="Jordan">hey 👋</sms>              <!-- a Spark dating-app message -->
<anon from="Lena" image="...">rate me?</anon>             <!-- Rated (18+) only -->
```

By default a short marker stays in the chat, e.g. *📱 Lena sent you $20.00.* You can change this to show the full text, or nothing. Searches, locations and anonymous posts never leave a marker.

## Replying

- **Text back on the phone:** type and press **↑** (or Enter). The text is queued, shown faded with "Sends with your next reply", and you can still edit or cancel it. Then send your normal chat message. **✈** (or Ctrl+Enter) queues the text and sends your chat reply in one go.
- **Or answer in your reply:** ↩ next to a text adds `*reads Lena's text: "…"*` to your chat box.
- If you delete your chat message, everything that went with it goes back into the outbox. Swiping or regenerating a reply swaps out the phone activity that came with it.

## Phone-only roleplay

Want the whole story to happen by text? Turn on **Roleplay through the phone** at the top of Messages (or in the phone's Settings, or type `/phone only`). It's saved per chat.

- **Texts send right away:** no need to write a chat reply. Your text goes into the chat as a transcript line (`📱 User → Lena: …`) and the character answers.
- **Characters answer by text.** The model is told to reply only with phone actions, and you'll see "typing…" while it writes. Whoever you texted answers, even if it's not the main character. If a reply comes back without any phone tags, it's turned into texts from that person, so the conversation stays on the phone.
- **Everything else works too:** calls go out immediately, and payments, orders and posts made outside a conversation wait in a **Send** bar at the bottom of the screen.
- **Centre stage:** the phone (or PC) sits in the middle of the screen over a dimmed chat. You can turn that off under *Settings → Centre the device in phone-only mode*.
- **Going back:** switch it off the same way, or type `/phone story`, to go back to telling the story in chat. The chat keeps a readable transcript either way.

## Photos

Pick a generator under **Extensions → Phone & PC → Photos**:

| Generator | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Automatic** | Uses SillyTavern's built-in **Image Generation** extension if it's set up (any backend it supports: A1111/Forge, ComfyUI, NovelAI, OpenAI, Pollinations, Stability…). |
| **Custom slash command** | Works with **other image extensions**. Enter any command that returns an image URL, e.g. `/imagine quiet=true {{prompt}}` or another extension's command. |
| **Pollinations.ai** | Free and needs no key. Prompts are sent to pollinations.ai. |
| **OpenAI-compatible API** | Endpoint, key and model (e.g. `gpt-image-1`, `dall-e-3`, or any compatible server). Images are saved into SillyTavern. |
| **Other extensions** | Extensions can add themselves to this list (see below). |

- **Automatic developing:** by default, photos sent in **texts** are generated automatically. Everything else shows a placeholder; tap it to develop. Set *Develop automatically* to *Everything* to generate all photos.
- **Images from other extensions:** if an auto image-generation extension attaches a picture to a chat message, the phone uses that picture for the photo.
- **Character appearance:** if a character has a character-specific prompt in the Image Generation settings, it's added to photos of them so they look like themselves.
- **Viewing:** tap a developed photo to view it full-size, open it, or **Retake** it.

### For extension authors

```js
// Register your generator. Return a URL, a data: URL or a markdown/HTML image.
window.stPhone?.registerImageProvider('my-gen', 'My Generator', async (prompt, info) => {
    return await myGenerate(prompt);
});
// Or wait for the phone to load:
document.addEventListener('stphone-ready', e => e.detail.registerImageProvider(/* … */));
```

`window.stPhone` also exposes `open()`, `close()`, `navigate(app)`, `generateImage(id)` and the current chat's `state`.

## Settings

| Section | Options |
| --- | --- |
| General | Enable, floating button, notifications, phone or PC, theme, chat marker style |
| Prompt injection | What goes into the prompt (texts and calls, posts, payments, orders, searches), how much, depth and role. Peek response length. |
| Photos | Generator, auto-develop, character appearance, use images from other extensions, photo prompt template |
| Pocket | Currency symbol, starting balance |
| Adult (18+) | Enable Rated (asks for confirmation), blur photos until tapped, reveal which character is behind an anonymous post |
| Prompts | Edit every prompt: tag instructions, phone-only roleplay, profile feeds, home feeds, browser, music, the rest of a peek, locate, store search, restaurants, dating profiles, Rated feed and comments |

## What generated content is based on

Peeks, Locate, store searches, restaurants, dating profiles and Rated feeds adapt to where the chat is:

| Chat | What the model is told to use |
| --- | --- |
| **No intro message** (empty chat, or the intro was cleared) | **Only the character card**: description, personality and scenario, included in the prompt. No opening scene, no alternate greetings, no invented history with `{{user}}`. |
| **Intro message only** | The character card plus the intro message that's actually showing. Other greetings from the card are never used. |
| **Ongoing chat** | The character card plus everything that's happened so far. |

Custom prompts can place this anywhere with `{{context}}`. If they leave it out, it's added at the end.

## Notes

- All phone data is stored in the chat's metadata, so every chat has its own phone.
- Peeking, store searches, restaurants, dating profiles and Rated feeds each use one quiet generation. If results get cut off, raise **Peek response length**.
- Group chats are supported: any member can text you, and you can peek at each one.
- Rated is for adults only. It's hidden unless you enable it, its prompts say every user is 18+, and its photos are blurred until tapped by default.
